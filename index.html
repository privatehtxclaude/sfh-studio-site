FAQ

Do the ads say they're AI?

We don't lead with it, and we're upfront if you ask. Platform AI-disclosure rules are your responsibility to apply when you publish; we'll tell you which ones apply to your placements.

Who owns the files?

Once paid, you get organic use and 90 days of paid-social use. Longer paid use, exclusivity and full buyouts are add-ons. See the terms.

What if I don't like it?

One revision round is included in every package. Daily volume includes ongoing direction changes.

Can you use my product photos or a real person's likeness?

Product photos, yes. We don't generate real people's likeness without written consent.
