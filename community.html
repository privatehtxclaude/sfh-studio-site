Ground rules

Program terms

Draft terms, not legal advice. 1) Always disclose that you're a paid partner when you share your link (for example "#ad" or "I earn a commission"), as the FTC requires. 2) No spam, no fake reviews, and no promises about results or income. 3) Commission is paid within 30 days after the customer's payment clears, and not on refunded or disputed payments. 4) Referred clients are credited to the member whose link they used first. 5) You're responsible for your own taxes. We may ask for a tax form before paying over the legal threshold. 6) Either side can end the arrangement at any time, and we can change the rates for future sales. 7) This is a single-level program. Commission is never paid for recruiting other promoters.
